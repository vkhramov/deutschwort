HOW THE WORDS ARE VERIFIED

Quality over quantity

The 2,500-entry set is selected from the Grundwortschatz multi-corpus dataset using frequency ranks and everyday word types. Russian meanings are matched from FreeDict German–Russian (Wiktionary/DBnary). Noun articles and plurals come from the vocabulary dataset. Verb cards use infinitives and show all six present-tense forms; preposition cards explain the governing case with examples. Grammar notes are based on Goethe-Institut and Deutsche Welle references. Entries are not all independently human-proofread, so this is a carefully filtered learning list rather than a certified dictionary. Open each word’s DWDS reference to check nuanced senses.
