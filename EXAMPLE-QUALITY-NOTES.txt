DeutschWort v8 — vocabulary example quality update

This release replaces a set of source-derived examples that were fragments, archaic, editorially annotated, misleading, or poor demonstrations of the target item. All 35 preposition entries were rewritten with clear contemporary sentences; translations were rewritten to match the German example. Additional high-risk examples and known translation issues were corrected.

Automated validation performed on all 2,500 entries:
- unique IDs and exact count
- non-empty German, English and Russian example fields
- JSON syntax and app data structure
- cache version bumped and progress migration retained

Important limitation: automated checks cannot certify every nuance of 2,500 translations. This is a targeted quality pass, not a claim of independent human proofreading of every entry. Source references remain attached to vocabulary entries; newly written example sentences are editorial examples, not direct quotations from those sources.
